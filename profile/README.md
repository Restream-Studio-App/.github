## 🚀 What It Is
Restream Studio for Windows is a web-based studio for producing live video without complicated software to install. It runs in your browser, so you can build a branded scene, bring in guests, and go live in just a few clicks. From the studio restream dashboard you connect your channels and manage everything in one place, making it easy for solo creators and small teams to look polished on air.

## ✨ Key Features
> Everything you need to run a professional live show lives inside one browser tab.

- 📡 **Broadcast Everywhere** — Send your live stream to YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, LinkedIn, and many more destinations at the same time.
- 👥 **Remote Guests** — Invite co-hosts and guests into your show through a shareable browser link.
- 🎨 **On-Brand Overlays** — Drop in your logo, name tags, backgrounds, and banners for a consistent look.
- 🖥️ **Screen Sharing** — Present slides, demos, and media directly within your live layout.
- 💬 **Unified Chat** — Read and highlight messages from every connected platform in a single stream.

## 📥 Setup
1. Click the **Download** button above to open Restream Studio in your browser.
2. Sign up for a free account or log in if you already use Restream.
3. Launch the Studio from your browser dashboard, where you can also grab the desktop app for Windows if it is offered for your account.

Note on "restream studio download": because the studio is web-based, getting started means opening it in your browser rather than running a large installer, so there is nothing heavy to set up before you can stream.

## 🎯 First Steps
1. Name your broadcast and pick the platforms you want to reach.
2. Turn on your camera and microphone, then choose a scene layout.
3. Add your logo and lower-thirds to brand the show.
4. Send guests the join link so they can appear on screen.
5. Hit **Go Live** and start streaming to all your channels together.

## ❓ Questions
**Is Restream Studio free?**
Yes, Restream Studio has a free plan that covers the essentials, with paid tiers available when you want more destinations, higher quality, and advanced branding.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
It runs in the browser, so any Windows 10 or Windows 11 machine with an up-to-date browser can use it.

**Is my data secure?**
Restream protects your account and broadcasts on its platform, and you decide which channels to connect and what to send live.
